Royal Marines Rifle Association Membership

The Royal Marines Rifle Association (RMRA) welcomes serving personnel, veterans, members of the Royal Marines family and supporters who share an interest in marksmanship, shooting sports and preserving the Corps' proud shooting traditions.

Full Member (£120)

For veterans, former service personnel, and members of the Royal Marines family who wish to participate fully in RMRA activities.

  • Full access to RMRA shooting activities, training and competitions.
  • Eligibility to compete in RMRA Championships and representative teams (subject to competition rules).
  • Eligibility to use firearms held on the RMRA Club Firearms Certificate, subject to RMRA policies and legal requirements.
  • Voting rights at Association General Meetings.
  • Eligible to stand for election to RMRA committees and appointments.
  • Support the continued development of Royal Marines marksmanship and shooting excellence.

Full Member (Serving) (£90)

Discounted Full Membership for serving Royal Marines and Royal Navy personnel.

  • All benefits of Full Membership.
  • Full access to RMRA shooting activities, training and competitions.
  • Eligibility to use firearms held on the RMRA Club Firearms Certificate, subject to RMRA policies and legal requirements.
  • Eligibility for RMRA representative teams and championships.
  • Voting rights and participation in Association governance.
  • Reduced subscription rate in recognition of current service.

Membership subject to verification of serving status.

Member (£60)

For shooters and supporters wishing to participate in RMRA activities.

  • Access to RMRA events and selected competitions.
  • Receive RMRA communications, news and updates.
  • Opportunity to participate in the RMRA shooting community.
  • Support the aims and objectives of the Association.

Associate (£40)

For family, friends and supporters of the Association.

  • Stay informed about RMRA activities and events.
  • Attend eligible social functions and selected events.
  • Support the promotion of shooting and marksmanship within the Royal Marines community.
  • Ideal for non-shooting supporters of the Association.

For membership enquiries, please contact secretary@rmra.org.uk.
