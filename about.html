ABOUT RMRA

Royal Marines Rifle Association • Established 1896

Founded in 1896, the Royal Marines Rifle Association (RMRA) has proudly supported excellence in marksmanship for well over a century. Built upon the traditions of professionalism, discipline and precision that define the Royal Marines, the Association exists to promote shooting, competition, training and fellowship amongst its members.

Throughout its distinguished history, RMRA members have represented the Corps at local, national and international shooting events, demonstrating the highest standards of skill, sportsmanship and firearms safety. The Association continues to provide opportunities for serving personnel, reservists, veterans and supporters to develop their shooting abilities and compete in a wide range of disciplines.

Today, the RMRA remains committed to encouraging participation at all levels, from newcomers discovering shooting sports for the first time to experienced competitors striving for excellence. Through organised competitions, training events and coaching, the Association helps preserve and advance the proud heritage of Royal Marines marksmanship.

IMG_9387.jpeg

As a member-led organisation, the RMRA serves as a focal point for the Royal Marines shooting community, fostering camaraderie, maintaining traditions and ensuring future generations can continue to enjoy and excel in the sport of shooting.

Our Mission

To promote and develop marksmanship throughout the Royal Marines family, preserve the Corps' rich shooting heritage, and provide opportunities for competition, training and fellowship while maintaining the highest standards of safety, responsibility and excellence.
